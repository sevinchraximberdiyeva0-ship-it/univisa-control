# Legal review input — not approval

Checked 16 November 2026. Authorized university legal counsel must determine the current requirements and approve the actual deployment and processing policy. No migration/visa deadline is represented as law in application code.

The primary legal source reviewed is [Uzbekistan Law ZRU-1125 of 26 March 2026](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/8104580?ONDATE=27.03.2026), effective 27 March 2026. It amends the personal-data law's database registration provisions and Article 27¹ concerning local storage and conditions for processing outside Uzbekistan. Its listed categories and permitted cross-border conditions require case-specific review. The LexUZ Russian page identifies itself as an unofficial translation; counsel should confirm the authoritative text and implementing measures.

Do not rely on older English summaries asserting a blanket localization rule. Counsel should classify student/staff identity records, uploaded scans, any biometric content, telecom-provider processing, Telegram disclosures, backups and logs; establish the lawful basis and notices; and verify registration, hosting, contracts, retention, access rights and incident-response duties. None of these decisions is approved merely by selecting a timezone or provider.

Record the approved policy, authority, scope and review date in controlled university governance records before production. The application currently leaves `legalApprovedAt` unset by default and has no public UI action that can falsely certify legal approval.
